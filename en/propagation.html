🌱 Plant Propagation

Propagation methods and instructions

🌸 Best time for propagation: Spring — Early summer

💧 💧 In water

The simplest and most visual method. Suitable for most vines and aroids.

📋 Step-by-Step Instructions

1
Cut a cutting with 1-2 nodes (where leaves grow).
2
Remove lower leaves so they don't rot in water.
3
Place in water. Add a piece of charcoal.
4
Wait for 2-5 cm roots, then plant in light soil.

🌿 Suitable for plants

Monstera deliciosa
Pothos / Scindapsus
Large-leaf Philodendron
Aglaonema (Variegated)

💡 Expert Tips

  • Use a dark container — roots love darkness.
  • Change water once a week.

🌱 🌱 In soil

For plants that don't tolerate water well or have delicate roots.

📋 Step-by-Step Instructions

1
Prepare cutting and let the cut dry for 30-60 min.
2
Dip the cut in rooting hormone (optional).
3
Plant in a light mix (peat + perlite 50/50).
4
Cover with a bag or jar to create a greenhouse.

🌿 Suitable for plants

Begonia maculata
Dracaena marginata

💡 Expert Tips

  • Ventilate the greenhouse every day.
  • Do not overwater — soil should be only slightly moist.

✂️ ✂️ Division

For bushy plants that form rosettes or have rhizomes.

📋 Step-by-Step Instructions

1
Remove plant from pot and clean soil from roots.
2
Carefully divide the bush by hand or with a sharp knife.
3
Each part should have its own roots and growth point.
4
Plant in separate pots.

🌿 Suitable for plants

Peace Lily (Spathiphyllum)
Calathea lancifolia
Anthurium
Snake Plant

💡 Expert Tips

  • Dust cuts with charcoal or cinnamon.
  • Keep in shade for the first week.

🌿 🌿 Air layering

For large woody plants (ficus, monstera) to get a large plant immediately.

📋 Step-by-Step Instructions

1
Make a cut on the stem or remove a ring of bark.
2
Wrap with moist sphagnum moss.
3
Secure with plastic wrap so the moss doesn't dry out.
4
When roots fill the wrap, cut and plant.

🌿 Suitable for plants

Monstera deliciosa
Philodendron selloum

💡 Expert Tips

  • Use moss soaked in stimulant solution.
  • Method helps preserve variegation.

👶 👶 Offsets

For plants that produce 'babies' on runners or at the base.

📋 Step-by-Step Instructions

1
Wait until the offset has its own root nubs.
2
Cut the offset from the mother plant.
3
Plant in a small pot with light soil.
4
Maintain high humidity initially.

🌿 Suitable for plants

Spider Plant (Chlorophytum)
Snake Plant
Guzmania

💡 Expert Tips

  • Don't separate offsets that are too small.
  • Chlorophytum can be rooted without cutting from the runner.
