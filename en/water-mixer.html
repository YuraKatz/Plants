🔬 Step-by-Step Guide: Preparing Water

1

💧 RO Water

Start with pure RO water. PPM should be near zero (0-10).

2

🧂 Add CalMag

Bring PPM to the target group value: A≈80, B≈120, C≈150.

💡 This base is the same for flushing and feeding!

3

🧪 Add Fertilizer

PPM will rise by 50-150 — this is normal! Fertilizer = salts = higher PPM. No need to adjust.

4

⏱️ Wait 2-3 minutes

Micronutrients don't dissolve instantly. pH may drop by 0.2-0.4 after full dissolution.

5

📊 Measure and adjust pH

Target pH: 5.8-6.5 (depending on the group). Adjust pH LAST — after all additives.

✓

🎉 Ready!

Water is ready for use. PPM after fertilizer: 130-300 is normal.
