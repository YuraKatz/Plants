📅 Seasonal Care

💡
Have lights 10+ hours? Ignore seasons!

Under artificial light, plants are always in 'summer'. Water and fertilize as in summer year-round. No need to reduce anything.

🏔️
Golan Heights (Katzrin): Special climate!

Winter is colder than on the coast (it snows!). Summer is hot but not extreme. Advice about cold windows in winter is relevant! Main enemies: dry air from heating in winter + air conditioning in summer.

🌸 Spring

March — May
💧 Watering
Gradually increase watering. Plants are waking up and starting active growth.
  • Water in the morning
  • Check moisture more often
🧪 Feeding
Start applying fertilizers. Begin with 1/4 dose, then transition to full.
  • First feeding in March
  • CalMag is mandatory
🪴 Tasks
Ideal time to get the collection in order.
  • Repot into fresh soil
  • Pruning and propagation
  • Shower to wash off dust

☀️ Summer

June — August
💧 Watering
Peak water consumption. Substrate dries very quickly.
  • Water 2-3 times a week
  • Monitor the wick
🧪 Feeding
Regular nutrition for active growth. Maximum doses.
  • Every watering is a feeding
  • Every 4th is a flush
☀️ Light
Danger of sunburn even through glass.
  • Shade southern windows
  • Move plants away from glass
🌡️ Temperature
Air conditioning — salvation for people, stress for plants.
  • Keep away from icy blast
  • Increase air humidity

🍂 Autumn

September — November
💧 Watering
Processes slowing down. Soil takes longer to dry.
  • Reduce watering frequency
  • Prevent stagnant water
🧪 Feeding
Preparation for dormancy. Lowering concentration.
  • Switch to 1/2 dose
  • Reduce nitrogen
🛠️ Tasks
Preparing for winter and cold.
  • Pest treatment
  • Move away from windows
  • Check window insulation

❄️ Winter

December — February
💧 Watering
Minimal need. Main risk — root rot.
  • Only after drying out
  • Room temperature water
☀️ Light
Catastrophic lack of light.
  • Supplemental light 10-12 hours
  • As close to window as possible
🌡️ Temperature
Cold from glass + dry heat from radiators.
  • Keep away from radiators
  • Insulate windowsills

🛑 ⚠️ Warning: Wet feet + Cold = Death

In winter in Katzrin, the windowsill can be +10°C. If roots are wet, rot starts in 24h. Water only when substrate is dry!

❄️ Special Plants in Winter

Alocasia
May drop leaves and go dormant (tuber). Don't discard!
Schlumbergera /
Water once a month or less. Cold wintering (+10...+15°C).
Adenium
Complete drying. Leaf drop is normal.
